Liquidation. An Euler v2 lending vault lends one asset against deposits in other vaults, its collateral vaults. For each accepted collateral the governor sets a borrow LTV, which bounds new borrowing, and a liquidation LTV, which sets when an account can be liquidated; the market facts show the liquidation LTV per collateral. A liquidation LTV cut can ramp down linearly. The governor acts without a timelock. An account may use several collaterals, so lenders are exposed to each in proportion to the debt it backs (the debt share). An account whose debt exceeds its collateral values weighted by their liquidation LTVs can be liquidated: a liquidator takes on debt in exchange for collateral. Debt left with no collateral can be socialized: total borrows fall by that amount, and so does every lender's assets per share.

Oracle. The vault prices collateral and debt through an oracle router in its unit of account. The router turns collateral vault shares into their asset, unwraps any further vault shares by their share rates, and applies a configured adapter (market feed, DEX price, Pendle PT rate, fixed rate, share or issuer rate, or two adapters chained). The router is fixed at creation, but its own governor can reconfigure it, changing what the oracle reads. Each market fact gives type, subtype and blind_to per collateral. The type says whether the oracle reads a market price of the collateral itself; blind_to names the move it cannot see (symbols may be upper-cased). An unseen move leaves the oracle price unchanged and triggers no liquidation. If the collateral's market value falls that way by more than 1 - LLTV, positions at the threshold hold collateral worth less than their debt, borrowers stop repaying, and lenders take the loss when the collateral is redeemed or the debt is written off.
- tracks_market: a fall of the collateral's market price is seen. direct: a market feed or DEX price of the collateral. collateral_is_numeraire: the collateral's asset is the unit of account, and the loan's own router path sets the price. pt_discount_then_underlying_market: a Pendle PT's TWAP discount against its underlying, then the underlying's market feed.
- exchange_rate: the collateral is priced through a share or issuer rate, never its own market price. A cut in the rate is seen; a market discount against the rate is not. The subtype says what prices the asset the rate converts into. underlying_at_own_feed: its own market feed. underlying_at_other_feed: another asset's feed, blind to a depeg against that asset. underlying_at_par: par, blind to a depeg of the underlying. issuer_reported_price: a rate the issuer reports; no market price is read. underlying_is_numeraire: the rate leads to the unit of account itself. pt_underlying_at_par, pt_underlying_at_other_feed: a PT's Pendle rate, then its underlying at par or at another asset's feed.
- fixed_price: constant_feed, the collateral's adapters return a constant, so no move is seen; fixed_peg_then_market_feed, the collateral is converted to another asset at a constant and priced by that asset's market feed, blind to a depeg against it.
- other: proxy_feed_other_asset, a feed of a different asset with no conversion, blind to a depeg against that asset; stored_price, a value an owner or keeper writes, moving only when updated.
- unresolved, unidentified_component: a step of the router path could not be identified; what the oracle sees is unknown.

Rate model. The governor sets the interest rate model and can replace it. The interest fee, set by the governor, is the share of interest that goes to the fee receiver instead of lenders; a new vault starts at 10 percent. With utilization u = total borrows / (cash + total borrows), supply APY = (exp(borrow APR) - 1) x u x (1 - interest fee). Added supply lowers u and the rate each unit earns.

Withdrawal limits. A lender can withdraw at most the vault's cash; a larger withdrawal is filled only up to it. Near 100 percent utilization cash stays near zero until repayment, liquidation or new supply. Debt never liquidated or socialized stays in total borrows and keeps accruing interest: the vault stays pinned, and book value and supply APY overstate what lenders can recover.
